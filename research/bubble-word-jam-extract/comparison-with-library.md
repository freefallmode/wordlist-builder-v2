# Bubble Word Jam compared with our library

Our side is the game export `data/export/wordlist-game-family.json`, library v493 (family export: adult and offensive entries left out). Bubble Word Jam's side is its 1,800 levels as this device plays them (`levels.json`). Words and theme names are compared ignoring case.

Built on 2026-11-26. Rebuild the numbers with `node compare_library.mjs ../../data/export/wordlist-game-family.json levels.json compare.json`.

## Size

| | Bubble Word Jam | Our library |
|---|---|---|
| Themes | 10,857 theme names | 3,133 playable themes (plus 120 group headings) |
| Different words | 21,605 | 57,281 root words (plus 4,119 aliases: UK/US spellings, synonyms, forms) |
| Words per theme in a level | always 4 | 4 |

## Words per theme

Bubble Word Jam counts each theme's whole word set across all its levels. Ours counts every root word linked to the theme.

| | Bubble Word Jam | Our library |
|---|---|---|
| Min | 4 | 4 |
| Max | 44 (Music) | 118 (religion, chemical elements) |
| Mean | 5.56 | 32.2 |
| Median | 4 | 33 |
| Themes with exactly 4 words | 7,818 (72%) | 70 (2%) |

Our theme sizes: 4 words: 70; 5-8: 208; 9-15: 361; 16-30: 669; 31-60: 1,689; 61+: 136.

**The two are built the opposite way round.** Bubble Word Jam has many small, hand-picked themes: most are a fixed set of four, and a theme returns with the same or a slightly different four. We have about a third as many themes, each a large pool that the game draws four words from, so a returning theme rarely repeats its four.

Bubble Word Jam reuses a smaller vocabulary across more themes; ours is wider and longer, with many more phrases and names.

## Structure and metadata

| | Bubble Word Jam | Our library |
|---|---|---|
| Sub-themes | 14% of theme slots have a parent theme (the sub-theme's name is also a bubble in the parent theme) | 64% of playable themes sit under another playable theme; up to 4 levels deep |
| Theme kinds | categories only | 2,541 category, 209 place, 184 property, 147 wordplay, 52 compound |
| Theme difficulty | none in the data (`levelDifficulty` is 0 everywhere) | 1-5 on every theme (mean 2.52) |
| Word metadata | the word, split pieces, special-bubble settings | part of speech, type, familiarity, word frequency (zipf), regions, UK/US variants, synonyms, link difficulty and typicality, sensitive/trademark flags |
| Levels | 1,800 fixed levels, about 12.6 themes each | generated by the game from the pools |

## Overlap

- **67% of Bubble Word Jam's words (14,497 of 21,605) are in our library**, plurals folded. Many of the rest are its sub-theme names used as bubbles (school subjects, card suits, makeup, building materials), adjectives and verbs (electric, herbal, wooden, breathe) and names (Peter Pan, Orwell, Wilde).
- **1,095 of its theme names (10%) match one of our theme names.** For those, 67% of Bubble Word Jam's words for the theme are in our theme too, and our theme is the larger in 98.5% of them.
