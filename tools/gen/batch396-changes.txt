Decisions (why):
  - Collisions were compared case-insensitively with every root and alias text (byText). The script stops on any collision not classified by hand.
  - Plain forms that are not English usage (Turkish dishes, "thank you" phrases, Spanish and Turkish color words, German numbers) are added, as
    rule 8 says, unless the plain form is a common English word of another meaning that nobody writes for this word (MISLEAD: role, fume).
    Not skipped though they are English words, because the plain form is the usual English spelling of this very word: Reunion (Réunion),
    entree, touche, Cortes, Pele, saute pan. Leon, Merida, Cadiz and the like are plain spellings of the same place.
  - German umlauts take the bare vowel (Dusseldorf, Jagermeister, Muller-Thurgau, funf, Marz), as plaincheck.mjs strips them and as the existing
    alias gluhwein (of glühwein) does; ß -> ss (dreissig).
  - People and places get the alias, typed as their root (person, place), as Simon Bolivar, Garcia Marquez, Sagrada Familia, Brasilia.
  - Locked-only roots (53 added) get the alias too; the game never shows an alias in a locked theme.
  - Rule 5 of the brief (alias of an alias): only roots get one. A plain text that is already an alias of another root is a collision (Tane, kaka, AU)
    or, for kumara, the same word (DUP).
