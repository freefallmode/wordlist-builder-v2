# Brief: find good entries that were left out for being over 20 letters

The game library limits entry texts to 20 letters (spaces and punctuation not counted) and 4 words. Earlier batches simply dropped or shortened well-known members whose names are longer, without recording them (examples: Hans Christian Andersen, German shorthaired pointer, sulphur-crested cockatoo, Elizabeth Barrett Browning, Alexander von Humboldt, Madagascar hissing cockroach). We want a candidate list of those.

Input: `node tools/gen/list-words.mjs <root> <n>` prints a chunk of up to 60 themes with all the words they already hold. For each theme think: which WELL-KNOWN, clearly correct members of this theme have a text longer than 20 letters (count letters only, use node to check) or that an earlier agent would have had to shorten, and are NOT already in the theme (also check likely short forms already present: e.g. "Andersen" present does not stop "Hans Christian Andersen" being listed as the full form; mention the short form in `note`)? Typical sources: full personal names, long animal/plant/breed names, long place/organisation/title names, long food or product names. Only real, standard names; no invented ones; at most 8 per theme, none for themes where nothing fits; skip trademark-heavy or doubtful names. Most themes will have none.

Output: `tools/gen/long/<root>-<n>.json`, a JSON array of `{"theme": "theme_id", "text": "Hans Christian Andersen", "type": "person|place|org|title|proper|common|acronym", "gloss": "" , "rec": 1-5 (how well known), "inRec": 1-5 (how readily placed in the theme), "note": "short form already in library: Andersen"}`. Verify with node that every text is over 20 letters (or 5+ words is not allowed: keep to 4 words), that the JSON parses and that theme ids exist. Use a uniquely named scratch script if needed. Do not edit anything else. Report in under 40 words: number of candidates.
