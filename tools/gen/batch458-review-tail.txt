Rama and Garuda, not split
- Rama 21970 [god]: its four themes (Hindu gods, Hinduism words, Diwali, legendary heroes) all mean one figure, the hero-king of the Ramayana, who is worshipped as Vishnu's avatar; Diwali celebrates his return. The hero and the god are the same figure, so there is no second sense to split off. Kept as one entry, unflagged (as v457).
- Garuda 21990 [eagle mount]: Hindu gods and winged beasts both mean the divine eagle of Hindu and Buddhist myth, Vishnu's mount. Kept as one entry, unflagged.
