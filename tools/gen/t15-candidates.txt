RULE USED
Base set: single-word entries (no space or hyphen) whose text is all lowercase, typed common (or untyped), with at least one theme link.
Measured signals first (tools/gen/t15-scan.mjs): capitalised twin entry with the same letters 888 hits; word in PROPER_WORDS 20;
loose origin-theme regex 5,538; twin + theme 261 (mostly turkey, olive, rose, bridge: false). All too loose to read as verdict lists.
Rule kept: the base set restricted to ~90 named origin themes (breeds of every animal, cheeses and their sub-themes, wines, grape varieties,
liqueurs, spirits, beers, teas, cocktails, sauces, dressings, dishes and their sub-themes, pastries, cookies, breads, pies, sausages, pizza
styles, melons, squashes, chili peppers, fabrics, prints, dances, hats, boots, shoes, clothes, words/foods/colours named after places or
people, ideologies named after people) = 1,387 words, read as bare word lists by theme (text only), plus lowercase entries linked to
place, language and nationality themes (regions, cities, islands, rivers, counties, languages, conquistadors) = 194, plus the 20 PROPER_WORDS
hits and the 888 twin words read as a bare list. Judged by own knowledge (Oxford and Merriam-Webster usage), then detailed with
tools/gen/t15-show.mjs. Result: 119 high, 36 medium.
History: tools/gen/t15-hist.mjs compared the text of every candidate id at 11 earlier library versions (before each case pass: v353, v355,
v359, v363, v367, v371, and the early fill versions): none was ever capitalised and then lowercased, so no candidate reverses an earlier
decision. Case logs (capmark, lone, case-fix, propword, style-case) and DESIGN-entries.md mention none of them as single words.
