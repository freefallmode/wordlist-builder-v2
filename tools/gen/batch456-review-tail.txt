Eggs: why it never played (game: 9 picks, 9 word-phase failures in every variant)
  Rated 1.5, eggs is picked at low levels, where the game shows full terms (W10). Seven of its nine usable full texts contain "egg" (egg yolk,
  duck egg, Easter egg, ostrich egg, egg carton, quail egg, free-range egg), and the game's B12 rule lets no two words of a level share a word,
  comparing full texts. So a level could hold at most eggshell, albumen and one "egg ..." word: three, never four. Senses, typicality, shows and
  sensitive flags were not the cause. The game-side fix (compare shown texts, W10) is agreed; this batch adds roe (a text without "egg") and makes
  egg white (familiarity 1) the root of albumen (4), shown "white". Yolk becomes a root (familiarity 1) with egg yolk
  its synonym alias (links move with their typicality: eggs 2, yellow things 2). Under the old full-text rule a level now gets eggshell, roe, yolk
  and one "egg ..." word: four.
