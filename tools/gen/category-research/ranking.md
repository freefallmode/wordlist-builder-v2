# Category-sorting word games: ranked by players

Data pulled 2026-11-18 from public pages only (no logins, no paid tools). Scope: mobile/web word games where the core loop is sorting words into hidden categories (bubble merge/sort, card-based "associations" word solitaire, tile/board grouping). NYT Connections excluded as requested; plain word-search/anagram/crossword games excluded.

## How to read the numbers

- **Android installs**: the exact install count Google embeds in the public Google Play page data (the page itself only shows the bucket, given in brackets, e.g. "10,000,000+"). Source link: `[GP]` = the play.google.com listing.
- **iOS ratings (US)**: rating count on the US App Store (`[AS]` = apps.apple.com listing; identical to the public iTunes lookup API). Apple does not publish installs. `all-storefront` = AppGoblin's ratings total across storefronts (`[AG]` = appgoblin.info page).
- **Android ratings**: the total rating count and average shown in the Play listing header.
- **Est. total installs (ESTIMATE)**: Android exact count + AppGoblin's *modelled* iOS install estimate. The iOS part is an estimate (AppGoblin derives it mostly from rating counts), so treat the total as a rough ranking key, not a fact. Rows are sorted by this column.
- **Launch date**: first-release date from each store (Android from the Play page, iOS from Apple).
- Category-source column is filled for the top 6 (plus any found incidentally). "none found" = searched, nothing usable.

## Checked and excluded

- **Wordscapes Solitaire** (PeopleFun, about 4.1M Android installs) and **Solitaire Word Match** (One Way Street, 421K): cards combine letters to spell words, with no category sorting.
- **Word Solitaire Journey** (Evrika, 285K): letter cards.
- **Word Screw** (Astrasen, 2.4M): you unscrew letters to spell words.
- **Word Bubble Puzzle** (People Lovin, 3.9M): bubbles are letters, used for spelling.
- **WordBubbles** (Apprope): word search.
- **Picture Blast Solitaire** (Gismart, Android 1.8M; Joyteractive iOS, 24K US ratings): picture jigsaw, no categories.
- **Association Match 4 Emoji** (Tripledot, 155K) and **HexMoji** (MagicLab, 136K): emoji only, no words. Category Sort (Lion) is also emoji-only, but it is kept in the table and flagged because of its size.
- **Serene Word Puzzle** (iOS, 18K ratings): word search.
- **Word Association!** (Funnerware, 2013): a different mechanic.
- No other Hitapps category game is above 20K installs: Association Cube (17K), Asocio (2.6K) and Card Shift (2.2K) are new or soft launches.

## Caveats

- Several games ship under different publisher names on each store: Word Solitaire Now!, Word Match and Word Associations Now! use the `losttemplegames` package on Android (MAXX GAMES) but Arrowise Studio or LE TECH on iOS. They were matched by title and description, not confirmed.
- Stamp Solitaire was **not** merged with the similarly named iOS app from Piti Studio.
- AppGoblin's iOS install figures are model estimates. AppGoblin's Android installs match the Play page data. The iOS rating counts from AppGoblin (all storefronts) are higher than the US-only counts from Apple.
- Rank order below about #20 is noisy: totals under 1M are dominated by the estimated iOS share.
